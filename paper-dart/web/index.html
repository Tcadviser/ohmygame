How to fold & fly

1 · Fold

Press and hold to run the crease along the dashed line. Keep your finger on the line and let go inside the green band. Five crisp steps make an S-grade dart that glides further.

2 · Launch

Hold to charge — the power needle sweeps. Drag up or down (W / S) to aim, then release to throw. The dotted arc shows where a level glide lands.

3 · Fly

Drag up / press W to raise the nose: you climb but lose speed. Drag down / S to dive: you gain speed. Paper has no engine, so height is your fuel.

4 · Land it

Green vents lift you, red vents push you down. Grab paperclips and stickers, dodge the props, and drop into the basket to clear the room.

Keyboard: Space fold / charge · W S aim & steer · Esc pause. Touch works everywhere.
